Stock horoscope button does

What it actually does right now
✅ Yahoo Finance — YES, it works
When you type a stock name and tap Analyse, it calls your /api/stock endpoint which fetches 5 years of daily OHLCV data from Yahoo Finance for free. It pulls:

Current price
All daily closes (~1250 data points)
Daily highs, lows, volumes
Timestamps

From those 1250 closes it auto-computes:

52-week high and low (last 252 trading days)
200-DMA (last 200 closes average)
50-DMA
5-year high and low
RSI (14-day) — calculated from actual price changes
MACD — EMA12 vs EMA26, histogram
Bollinger Bands — 20-day mean ± 2 standard deviations
5-year range percentile — where price sits in 5 years of history
Volume signal — recent 5-day volume vs 20-day average

Zero manual input needed. All 7 indicators computed automatically.

✅ What it shows an investor
For BUY decisions:

Score ≥65 + 4/6 layers bullish = favourable window
RSI below 30 = oversold, historically good entry
Price in bottom 20% of 5-year range = multi-year accumulation zone
200-DMA slope rising = long-term uptrend intact
MACD crossover from negative to positive = momentum building
Shukla Paksha + Ekadashi/Purnima tithi + Mercury direct = auspicious timing
Realistic upside target (+X%) and time horizon shown

For SELL / EXIT decisions:

Score below 50 = avoid or exit
RSI above 70 = overbought, pullback risk
Price near 5-year high = stretched valuation
MACD histogram turning negative = momentum fading
Rikta tithi (4, 8, 13) = classical prohibition, don't hold new entries
Mercury retrograde = IT/banking entries likely to reverse
Stop-loss % shown — if stock drops that much, exit immediately
